About

Bioinformatics researcher and computational biologist with a PhD in Bioinformatics from the University of Tehran. My research focuses on learning-based computational modelling of tumor cell migration at spatiotemporal single-cell resolution. I have broader experience in single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, computational biology, and complex biological network analysis. I combine formal training in computer science with biological data analysis and extensive experience in higher education.
